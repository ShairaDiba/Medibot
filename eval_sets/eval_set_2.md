## Question 3 - DEF-005 (Definition)

**Query given to MediBot:** What is hypertension?

**MediBot's answer:**

1) Hypertension is high blood pressure, the force of blood pushing against the walls of arteries as it flows through them.
2) Blood pressure rises when the heart beats and falls when the heart relaxes; narrow (constricted) arteries increase pressure, while dilated arteries lower it.
3) It affects the cardiovascular, respiratory, psychiatric, and hematological systems.
4) Classified as a severe condition.
5) All ages can be affected.
6) It is not contagious.
7) Possible symptoms include headache, visual disturbances, anxiety, and shortness of breath.
8) In 90-95% of cases the cause is unknown (primary or essential hypertension); temporary rises from activity or stress are not considered hypertension.
9) Diagnosis is made by regular blood-pressure measurement with a sphygmomanometer.
10) Treatment involves lifestyle changes (reduce salt and fat intake, lose weight, exercise, quit smoking, limit alcohol, manage stress) and antihypertensive medicines; many therapeutic procedures are listed in the dataset.
11) There is no cure, but hypertension can be well controlled with proper treatment.
12) Prevention focuses on diet, weight management, physical activity, smoking cessation, alcohol moderation, and stress management.

This is educational information, not a diagnosis.

## Question 7 - DEF-013 (Definition)

**Query given to MediBot:** Define abscess

**MediBot's answer:**

1) An abscess is a localized collection of pus that forms when tissue turns to liquid as a result of infection, showing the classic signs of inflammation-heat, swelling, redness, and pain.
2) Most abscesses are septic (caused by germs) and can occur anywhere in the body; sterile abscesses are less common.
3) As the infection progresses, chemical digestion liquefies more tissue, allowing the abscess to spread.
